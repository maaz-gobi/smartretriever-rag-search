# SmartRetriever — Use Cases

Real-world problems SmartRetriever solves.

## Enterprise Knowledge Base

Internal documentation, policies, and process docs are useless if nobody can find them. SmartRetriever makes the whole collection answerable in plain language.

## Research & Literature Review

Researchers working across hundreds of papers or reports can query the entire corpus at once and get sourced answers instead of reading everything manually.

## Legal & Contract Search

Search large sets of contracts, policies, and regulatory documents with both semantic understanding and exact-term precision.

## Consulting & Client Reporting

Consultancies can retrieve relevant findings from source material and compile them into structured client reports — faster turnaround, consistent format.

## Compliance & Audit

Find every document touching a specific topic, requirement, or clause — hybrid search ensures exact references aren't missed.

## Customer Support Knowledge

Turn product manuals, help docs, and past tickets into a system that surfaces accurate, sourced answers for support teams.
